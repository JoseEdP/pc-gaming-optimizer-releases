Privacy Policy

PC Gaming Optimizer for Windows · Last updated October 28, 2026

PC Gaming Optimizer is made by Codenectar. This policy explains what the app does with information on your PC. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing about you anywhere.

What stays on your PC

To do its job, the app reads system information such as your CPU, GPU, memory, Windows version, installed games and current Windows settings. It stores your settings, applied tweaks, undo snapshots, benchmark results, backups and a log of which features you've used in local files (under %APPDATA%\pc-gaming-optimizer and %LOCALAPPDATA%\PCGamingOptimizer). None of this is uploaded.

What the app sends over the network

  • Update checks. The app checks GitHub for new versions shortly after it starts and every 6 hours, and downloads updates from GitHub. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to GitHub. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Links you click. Some buttons, such as driver download pages, open a website in your browser. The app doesn't send anything with them.

There is no telemetry, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or account.

Sharing codes and exports

You can export optimization profiles and benchmark results as files or PCGO1: codes. They're created on your PC and only go where you choose to send them. Both kinds of export include your CPU and GPU model names.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app sends, this page will be updated before that version is released, and any new data sharing will be off until you turn it on.
